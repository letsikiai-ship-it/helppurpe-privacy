HelpPurple Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 October 2026

HelpPurple is an application developed by Letsiki & Co to help women access information about gender-based violence, personal safety, and support services.

1. Information We Collect

HelpPurple does not collect, request, or store personal information. The app provides informational resources and guidance on personal safety and accessing support.

The app does not require users to create an account, provide an email address, or provide a phone number.

2. How the App Works

HelpPurple provides information about safety planning, knowing your rights, emotional support, and emergency services.

When you select an emergency contact option, the app opens your phone's dialler so you can review the number and decide whether to make a call. HelpPurple does not itself make the call or collect the information you provide during it.

3. Sharing of Information

HelpPurple does not collect or share users' personal information with third parties.

When you choose to contact an emergency service or another support organisation, that interaction takes place outside the app and is subject to the relevant service provider's privacy practices.

4. Data Storage and Security

HelpPurple does not maintain a database of users' personal information. The app is designed to provide information directly to users without requiring them to submit personal details.

5. Permissions and Your Privacy

HelpPurple does not require users to provide personal information to access its informational features. Any information you choose to share with external services is handled by those services according to their own policies.

6. Children's Privacy

HelpPurple does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app provides informational resources and encourages users to seek assistance from appropriate support services when needed.

7. Changes to This Policy

If the app's functionality or privacy practices change, this policy may be updated. Any revised policy will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
