HelpPurple Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 October 2026

HelpPurple is an application developed by Letsiki & Co to help women access information and support related to gender-based violence. We respect your privacy and aim to handle personal information responsibly.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, HelpPurple may process information you voluntarily provide, such as messages, reports, contact details, or information you submit when requesting assistance.

The app may also process technical information required for its operation. Information such as location, emergency contact details, or device permissions is used only if the relevant feature is implemented and you grant the necessary permission.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide the app's features and support resources.
  • Help you access assistance or submit information, where available.
  • Maintain app security and troubleshoot technical problems.
  • Improve the app's reliability and functionality.

3. Sharing of Information

HelpPurple does not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers needed to operate the app, or with other parties when you request a service, when required by law, or where otherwise legally permitted. Any sharing depends on the features you use and the services integrated into the app.

4. Storage and Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the app. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Information may be stored on your device or on external services, depending on how the app is implemented.

5. Your Choices

You can control app permissions through your device settings. You may also contact us to ask about personal information associated with you and request its correction or deletion, subject to applicable law and any necessary retention requirements.

6. Children's Privacy

HelpPurple is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or its data practices change. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
